# Guarded scattering closure and a clocked renewal rank

An analytic all-parameter proof is supported by exact finite interface certificates.

## 1. Composable protected interfaces

A finite library is closed under arbitrarily many successive encounters,
including repeated changes between northeast and southeast standard highways.
It handles every bit of the history generated by earlier encounters, rather
than restarting on a blank field. A moving guard proves that retired history
cannot return. The existing strict clock supplies a literal-step countdown
inside each recovery; an explicit marker count supplies the outer event rank.

The 30-rule, two-orientation composition certificate supplies an all-length
induction and a clocked rank for the specified protected interfaces.

The rules require an incoming standard phase, a specific mature local wake,
a single fresh marked cell, and sufficiently separated subsequent markers.
They do not cover arbitrary interrupted preludes or an arbitrary westward
return into old debris. The protected boundary is the essential hypothesis,
not a property supplied by the global clock alone.

## 2. Literal convention and finite certificate data

A state is the COMPLETE finite black set B, position p, and heading h in
N,E,S,W=0,1,2,3. White turns right, black turns left, then flip and move.
Time t is before update t. All markers are installed at time zero. There is
no intervention between encounters and no physical deletion of old cells.

Rotate the repository's thirteen-cell canonical template by 180 degrees:

    K={(0,6),(1,5),(0,5),(2,4),(0,4),(2,3),(-1,3),
       (1,2),(-1,2),(1,1),(2,0),(1,0),(0,0)}.

The start pose is (0,0), EAST. Its standard word w, length 104, and displacement
d=(2,2) are reproduced and infinitely checked in
[check.py](../verification/guarded_scattering/check.py). Let P_i,H_i
be its pre-update phase positions and headings, 0<=i<104, with P_0=(0,0).
Let R_0 be identity and R_1(x,y)=(y,-x), a clockwise rotation, NOT reflection.
The two incoming orientations have displacement d_k=R_k d, k=0,1.

A phase j is fresh when its cell has no earlier visit in the two-sided planned
standard geometry. For P_j-P_i=a*d, an earlier visit exists exactly when

    a<0, or a=0 and i<j.

The finite test yields 22 fresh phases:

    13,14,15,27,28,29,32,38,45,46,47,
    55,56,57,60,61,62,70,71,72,75,89.

This is geometry of a certified periodic plan; it does not assume an infinite
physical seed or replace the intrinsic history of an arbitrary input.

## 3. Exact mature-wake restriction and guarded recovery

For a rule (k,j,g), put A_i=R_k(P_i-P_j), d_k=(2,+/-2), and
u_i=0 if i<j, u_i=-1 otherwise. Define the finite set by XOR:

    M_(k,j,g) = XOR over i=0..103, ceil((g-A_i.x)/2)<=a<=u_i
                    of {A_i+a*d_k}.

It is the restriction to x>=g of all writes before the collision in the mature
incoming standard plan. The positive x drift makes the displayed ranges finite.
Freshness implies 0 is not in M. The certificate kernel is

    X_r=(M union {0}, (0,0), (H_j+k) mod 4).

For each of the 30 rules the checker proves the following finite obligations:

1. The literal t-update execution ends at the declared COMPLETE kernel field
   and pose; a separately written integer-vector simulator agrees on every
   turn and on the complete endpoint.
2. Every recovery read has x>=g.
3. Both exact infinite-tail verifiers accept the declared standard phase from
   that endpoint. Its drift has positive x component 2, and every phase read
   in the first tail period has x>=g.

The two infinite tests are not finite repetition heuristics. One evaluates,
for each phase ray, all onset thresholds for previous writes and all exceptions
from the finite initial black field. Parity is constant between those finitely
many breakpoints, including the last infinite interval. The other orders all
visits to a fixed translation-class cell: writing its coordinate as m*d, a
phase at longitudinal index z_i visits at time 104*m+(i-104*z_i), when m>=z_i.
Those visits must alternate colours beginning with the actual initial colour.
It checks every change of the active list and every finite-field exception.
The two tests share the integer geometry primitives; they are two independently
formulated all-future checks, not two independently maintained software stacks.

**Guard lemma.** Add ANY finite black field G contained in x<g to X_r. The
entire future read/turn sequence remains that of X_r, and G is never touched.

**Proof.** Until a first x<g read, first-difference induction gives the certified
recovery and then its certified tail. Both stay in x>=g, so no first such read
exists. This proves invisibility instead of assuming it. G remains physically
present. The same reasoning holds until a later, explicitly planned marker
interrupts the tail. Permanent protection across such interruptions requires
the nesting argument in the next section. QED.

## 4. Finite arithmetic certificate for every compatible pair

Suppose rule r has output displacement d_k, phase a and endpoint v_r relative
to its marker. To place a later phase-j marker after n full periods plus the
phase adjustment, its position relative to the previous marker is

    q'=v_r+(n+1_[j<a])*d_k+R_k(P_j-P_a),
    flight updates=104*n+((j-a) mod 104).                  (1)

Use n>=100. This deliberately loose threshold is not asserted optimal.
For the next rule s with guard g_s, its absolute half-plane starts at q'.x+g_s.
The certificate checks that this boundary is strictly to the right of:

- every previous recovery read and every black cell of its complete kernel
  endpoint;
- the previous guard;
- the entire synthetic standard past before that endpoint.

The last maximum is finite and, relative to v_r, equals

    max_i [R_k(P_i).x-R_k(P_a).x + (0 if i<a else -2)].     (2)

Earlier epochs only decrease x by further multiples of 2. Formula (2), rather
than a simulation horizon, proves that all omitted pre-output planned writes
are outside the next half-plane.

There are exactly 448 compatible ordered rule pairs. At n=100 all pass, with
minimum strict-boundary slack **122** and minimum marker x displacement **184**.
For larger n, the left-hand boundary increases by 2(n-100) and the earlier
objects do not change. Thus the same inequalities hold for EVERY n>=100.
The 16 initial NE choices also pass; the minimum initial boundary slack is 160.

Future markers cannot interrupt an earlier recovery because they lie strictly
beyond its full read footprint. Nor can they interrupt an earlier free flight:
the x overshoot of a periodic flight before its target is bounded by twice the
maximum of the two finite canonical x spans plus 2, which is less than 184.
The checker tests this bound against every pair. Consequently chronological
order of the planned encounters is proved; it is not an extra all-future
assumption hidden in the definition of an admissible train.

### Why the output really re-enters the same input family

At the next collision half-plane, all previous endpoint black cells and all
previous retired debris are outside. Inside, only the newly generated standard
writes and the remaining preinstalled markers occur. By (2), adding back the
synthetic pre-output past changes nothing there. Therefore those newly generated
writes restrict to EXACTLY M_(k,j,g_s). The current marker adds its single black
cell; more distant markers remain untouched. This is equality of the COMPLETE
inside field, not equality on a fixed-radius sample or merely on reference rays.
It proves closure of the interface needed by the next rule.

All later guards strictly increase. Between guards, the certified tail stays
to the right of the previous one; each later recovery stays to the right of
its newer guard. Hence every retired western region is permanently protected
under the whole composed run, even though later deflections occur.

## 5. All-length, multi-encounter convergence theorem

Choose ANY finite directed word r_1,...,r_m in the displayed two-state graph,
starting NE, and ANY integers n_1,...,n_m>=100. Starting with p_0=(0,0), phase
0 and NE orientation, apply (1) recursively to obtain q_i, then set

    p_i=q_i+v_(r_i),

and use rule r_i's certified outgoing phase and orientation for the next step.
This is a finite, effective prescription using integer arithmetic and the fixed
rule table. Define the literal initial state

    B_0=K union {q_1,...,q_m} union G,
    p_0=(0,0), h_0=E,

where G is ANY finite set in x<-1. The markers, K and G are disjoint, so
|B_0|=13+m+|G|. No marker is installed later during the run.

**Theorem (guarded scattering trains).** Every such state encounters the
markers in the prescribed order, executes all prescribed recoveries with its
complete history retained, and reaches permanent standard P104 by time

    T=sum_(i=1..m) [104*n_i+((j_i-a_(i-1)) mod 104)+t_(r_i)].  (3)

For m=0 take T=0. The final direction is the graph's final NE or SE orientation.

**Proof.** The canonical tail has minimum read x=-1, so G cannot affect its
first flight. The initial-boundary inequalities remove K and every synthetic
pre-start write from the first collision half-plane. Freshness and the flight
overshoot bound give the first encounter and its complete restricted kernel.
Apply the guard lemma up to the next encounter. Section 4 proves both that
this next encounter is not premature and that it again has exactly the required
restricted kernel. Induct on the finite rule word. Nested guards keep every
retired part invisible throughout all remaining recoveries. After the last
marker there is no further perturbation ahead and the final infinite-tail
certificate applies with the entire western history still present. Summing the
actual flight and recovery lengths gives (3). QED.

This is not just checking more finite seeds: m, all gap parameters, and the
finite western history are unbounded. The proof is the closed-interface
induction, not extrapolation from the replay tests.

## 6. Transfer of the strict clock into an actual renewal rank

These renewal checkpoints are recovery boundaries of this new scattering
program. They are NOT silently identified with PG secondary gates or S_D events.
The following rank is for the certified train class, not arbitrary finite states.

During a recovery choose its finite read rectangle Q, including its endpoint.
Exterior colours remain unchanged. Let s=x+y-c, with integer c centering the
rectangle's diagonal range, and M=max_(p in Q)|s(p)|. With the invariant sigma
from [the clock proof](../../clock_escape/PROOF.md), use the scaled integer clock

    C_Q(X)=-5*sigma*sum_(q in B intersect Q) chi(q)
                              *(4^(M+s(q))+4^(M-s(q))) + G(p,h),

where G=2*(4^(M+s)-4^(M+1-s)) for h=N,E and
G=2*(4^(M-s)-4^(M+1+s)) for h=S,W. It is 5*4^M times the r=4 clock, with the
unchanged outside contribution subtracted. For every internal literal update,

    C_Q(FX)-C_Q(X)>=2*4^M.                                (4)

A computable upper bound U_Q for all internal colourings and poses is

    5*sum_(q in Q)(4^(M+s(q))+4^(M-s(q))) + max_(p,h in Q)|G(p,h)|.

Thus

    rho_Q(X)=ceil((U_Q-C_Q(X))/(2*4^M))                    (5)

is a nonnegative integer which strictly decreases on every internal recovery
update. Each stored finite recovery checks (4), (5), and the complete endpoint
clock again. The universal identity is imported from the existing clock proof;
these finite checks validate the particular representation and convention.

The whole certified computation has a lexicographic rank in N x {0,1} x N:

    (number of still-unread input markers, mode, local fuel),

with recovery mode 1 and flight mode 0. During flight, local fuel is the exact
number of updates to the next marked read, computed by (1). Reading that marker
decreases the first coordinate, permitting a reset to recovery mode. During
recovery, use rho_Q; returning to flight decreases mode, permitting the next
flight fuel to be reset. After the last recovery the certificate program halts.
This is a rank on the state with its finite checked train description, not a
claimed all-input physical-state rank. It stops at certification; the actual
ant then continues forever along its highway.

The logical role of closure is crucial: generated historical black cells do
not create further unaccounted marker obligations because their possible
interactions have been absorbed in the verified recovery and then guarded.
Without that proof, counting original markers would repeat the failed general
seed-size induction. The clock alone cannot supply that guard.

large field dumps are unnecessary. Kernel checking is independent of the

whole-repository test suite, or Lean file is changed or represented as rerun.

**Still missing for general HC:** a closure theorem for configurations outside

No assertion is made that other types are impossible or nonconvergent. Nor is
it proved that an arbitrary finite input ever enters this train interface.
The global autonomous-regeneration question remains in force.
