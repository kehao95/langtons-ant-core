\section{Fixed standard word}\label{frame:sec:word}
The following two lines concatenate to the chosen $104$-letter reference:
\begin{center}\small\ttfamily
RRLLLLRLRRRRLRRRRLLLLRLRRRRLRLLRRLLLLRRLLRRRRLLRRLRL\\
LRLLRLRLLRRRRLLRLLRRRRLLRRRRLLRLRRRRLRLLLLRRRRLRRLRR
\end{center}
The companion control reproduces the complete blank-plane state after $9977$ updates, with $715$ black cells and pose $(-15,10),W$. Its next $104$ letters are a cyclic phase of this word; both all-future checks certify that phase on the complete state. Thus ``standard'' is fixed by the classical blank reference, not by the number $104$ alone. The word is primitive by a direct finite shift test.
