previous 739-case run. Its original source archive and finite records are absent
from this checkout. Historical reported success is not a current reproducible proof.

canonical word, complete wake recurrence, permanent canonical tail and case-count
arithmetic. They validate the inputs of the reduction, not its perturbed recoveries.
