# Contribution and literature comparison — 15 October 2026

The paper's question is existence of some finite-support periodic realization
for a supplied finite wake, non-axis drift and heading, with no input period.

| Primary source | Established content | Relation to this manuscript |
|---|---|---|
| Jillhewar, [Finite-Support Periodic Highways](https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-10516269/v1), [pinned manuscript](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Atharva12456/langtons-ant-highway/76a7e234e577d992d086c204c3dea00ae72cf10a/paper/main.tex) | Exact finite-word realization and finite seed; diagonal extremal rigidity; Appendix D's width-four bound N<=14m | The word criterion and extremal argument are attributed. Our contribution is explicit general wake-dependent cutoffs and their inverse-synthesis application. |
| Same author's [width-four note](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Atharva12456/langtons-ant-highway/76a7e234e577d992d086c204c3dea00ae72cf10a/research-notes/width4_research_2026-07-21.md) | Bounded-period search deduced from a special geometric period bound | The general strategy of bounding then enumerating words is established. |
| Ke, [An Ant on the Clock](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23341603), [companion revision](../../clock_escape/paper/main.tex) | Integer clock, quantitative escape and complete-state orbit algorithms | Primary ownership of the shared clock. The wake manuscript gives its diagonal period application and reciprocal-moment extension. |
| Etse, [How Long Can the Escaping Ant Be Confined?](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.26677v1) | Finite-domain escape and geometric visit estimates | Bounding exit of a given state differs from synthesizing a periodic state from its wake. |

The fixed source revision above was inspected on 15 October 2026; it also
contains later transverse exclusions beyond the v1 abstract. Those exclusions
are not dependencies of our cutoff theorem. This review makes no global
priority claim. The manuscript keeps the positive standard witness and the
known excluded-width negative case explicitly as implementation controls.

The clock revision and wake first release should be coordinated: cite the
actual clock revision DOI once released, while retaining the current
self-contained derivation and companion-source reference in review copies.
