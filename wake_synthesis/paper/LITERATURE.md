# Literature and contributions

The main contribution remains thin-wake uniqueness at full drift `(2,-2)`,
growth 12, width 10 and at most one odd class on each interior level. It derives
the prescribed standard layer profile and complete wake. General non-axis
wake-dependent budgets and terminating inverse synthesis remain in the same
version chain; the mixed and general quadratic tests refine that inverse problem.

[Jillhewar's source at 76a7e234e577](https://github.com/Atharva12456/langtons-ant-highway/blob/76a7e234e577d992d086c204c3dea00ae72cf10a/paper/main.tex)
is a fixed version of the [author manuscript](https://atharva12456.github.io/langtons-ant-highway/paper.pdf).
The revision already cited by this project contains Theorems 9.5, 9.7 and 9.9,
excluding diagonal widths four, six and eight. The introduction places the
conditional uniqueness result within the smallest surviving width. These width
exclusions are context, not added premises of our proof.

Theorem 3.1/Remark 3.2, extremal rigidity, signed residues and the inherited
stable-rank factorization retain their original attribution. The CC BY prose
attribution and the MIT licenses on the two byte-identical imported audit scripts
remain under `verification/periodic_growth_closure/third_party/`.
[Gajardo (2003)](https://doi.org/10.46298/dmtcs.2312) owns the symbolic/forbidden-word
background; [clock 0.4.0](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23402379) owns the shared
clock. The width-four arithmetic budget in the pinned source is prior context
for deriving finite search ranges.

[Wake 0.1.0](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23402422) and
[0.2.0](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23405441) are earlier publications in the
same DOI version chain. Current 0.3.0 has weaker wake
hypotheses and integrated parity refinements. Neither the literature comparison
nor the theorem establishes unrestricted highway uniqueness.
