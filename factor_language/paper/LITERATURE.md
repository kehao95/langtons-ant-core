# Literature and contributions

The contribution is the concrete ten-factor physical classification and its
all-parameter fold exclusion, quantitative attraction, exact J10 algorithms,
finite-horizon supply and mass–defect inequalities. The nine-factor mass
threshold and ten-factor arbitrary-colouring attraction threshold are distinct.

Primary comparisons:

- [Gajardo (2003)](https://dmtcs.episciences.org/articles/2312), Sections 3 and 6:
  symbolic projection and forbidden words are established methodology.
- [Gajardo–Mazoyer (2007)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tcs.2006.10.004): [author manuscript](https://www.ing-mat.udec.cl/investigacion/paginas-pp/download.php?file=pp05-10.pdf),
  Section 6, Proposition 8 and Theorems 1–2: trace languages and return tours.
  The bounded-return equivalence in Proposition 8 is for a tape on Z.
- [Gajardo–Lutfalla–Rao (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11047-025-10018-9): use
  the formal Natural Computing citation, with [arXiv v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2409.10124v1)
  as the accessible author text. Its main multiplicity results use generalized rules.
- [Jillhewar, fixed revision](https://github.com/Atharva12456/langtons-ant-highway/blob/76a7e234e577d992d086c204c3dea00ae72cf10a/paper/main.tex),
  Theorem 3.1 and Remark 3.2: supplied-word realization remains attributed.
- [Clock 0.4.0](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23402379) owns the shared escape tools.

A finite graph alone does not give the physical fold exclusion. The universal
parameter argument, exact modes, sharp coefficients and finite-word certificates
identify the mathematical increment. The archive constructions stay with this
paper; the independently parameterized fold–consumer resource image belongs to
Counting Defects.
