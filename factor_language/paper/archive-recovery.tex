\subsection{Complete recovery of the producer family}
The same $10n+41$-black field $B_n$ admits an exact infinite continuation.
Thus the producer, its later consumer and the eventual highway can be studied
on one complete autonomous trajectory.

\textbf{Theorem (complete archive recovery).}

first permanent entry and $E_n$ its total number of future intrinsic $J_{10}$
events, including the nine windows across entry. Then

$(16,8),\mathrm N$, with drifts $(-2,-2)$ and $(2,2)$. In both infinite

\textit{Proof.}

$(0,4n+9),\mathrm W$ with complete mass $22n+61$. The supplied grammar
continues this exact state with 21 variable loops and 22 fixed words in the
even regime, or 27 variable loops and 28 fixed words in the odd regime.
The loop drifts are vertical, of magnitude four or eight; their lengths and
affine repeat counts are derived from their stored letters. Summing them
gives additional durations $474n+6524$ and $636n+10897$.
The grammar is exposed by the supplement's decompressor, together with word
lengths and hashes.

For execution, write $n=2k+10$ or $2k+17$. Split displacement-four loop
indices by parity and represent physical sites as $(x,\rho+8r)$,
$0\le\rho<8$. Each phase becomes a record with an affine integer interval
$L(k)\le r<U(k)$ and an affine time $ak+br+c$. Input cells have the same
representation. All source cells and all reads are included. Endpoint
crossings split the even domain into $k=0$ and $k\ge1$, and the odd domain
into $k=0$, $k=1$ and $k\ge2$. These partitions are derived by comparing
all affine endpoints. Endpoint tests, together with the slope on an unbounded
slab, establish every order inequality over its entire domain.

Within each spatial band the source colour and the ordered list of reads
are fixed. The checker verifies strict chronology and alternates the actual
colour at each read. This covers 11,371 spatial bands, 91,849 read checks
and 80,982 chronological inequalities. Induction over time proves the whole
script for every parameter. Source XOR prefix reads gives its complete
terminal field, including unread cells.

All pre-tail black cells have $x$ coordinates in $[-24,15]$ or $[-24,20]$.
For standard phase positions $q_i$ and drift $d$, the colour at $q_i+ed$

Nine inverse updates supply the true initial context. For each variable loop,

Mass follows independently by summing right minus left turns. Finally, the
