## 1. What was tested

The existing first-J10 macro preserves complete fields and has a hull bound
relative to the COMPLETE input support. The earlier S_D macro has the stronger
endpoint-tube property. Transferring that property to J10 would have made fixed
spatial defect cores much easier to exclude. The transfer is false.

A second proposal was to regard defects as consuming memory written at earlier
defects, leaving defect-free intervals as harmless transport. That literal
last-writer premise is also false. A completely J10-free interval can manufacture
arbitrarily many black lifetimes subsequently consumed at J10 events, even after
EVERY initial black lifetime has been consumed. This is not an objection to
arbitrary bookkeeping genealogies, or to a rank which already charges latent
resources in the complete field.

The words A and B below are inherited from the non-simple component in
[ten-factor attraction](ten-factor-attraction.md). The construction and checks
here use literal dynamics and per-cell alternation; they do not rerun or depend
on the old 6,605-word graph enumeration or its attraction proof.

## 2. Exact theorem

Use the unchanged HC convention: white/R, black/L, flip then move; time is
pre-update; headings N,E,S,W are 0,1,2,3. J10 uses the TRUE preceding nine turns
recovered by the inverse, followed by the current turn. Its whitelist is the
84 cyclic ten-factors of the fixed standard word W.

For every integer n >= 1, the explicit complete finite input X_n in Section 3
has 10n+41 black cells, head (-1,0), heading W. Put

    T_n = 56n+167,                 e_n = 56n+168,
    V_n = B[9:] A^n B A^(n-1),
    P   = RLRRRRLRRRRLLLLRLRRL.                         (1)

Here B[9:] deletes the first NINE letters, and P has length twenty.

**Theorem 1 (complete finite execution).** The literal orbit of X_n starts with

    V_n P^(n+1) RLL.                                   (2)

The three complete checkpoint fields are the finite low/bulk/high profiles
`return`, `pulse_end`, and `final` in the immutable witness. They are not cleaned
fields, quotient states or samples of a comparison region. Their exact data are
part of the certificate checked by the all-parameter argument in Section 4.
Their times, poses and complete masses are:

| Checkpoint | Time | Head | Heading | Complete black mass |
|---|---:|---|---|---:|
| after V_n | 56n+167 | (0,4) | N | 18n+58 |
| after P^(n+1) | 76n+187 | (0,4n+8) | N | 22n+62 |
| after RLL | 76n+190 | (0,4n+9) | W | 22n+61 |

Every initial black cell has been read at least once BEFORE T_n. Thus no black
lifetime already present at time zero survives to T_n. This does NOT say those
coordinates stay white: many have since acquired new black lifetimes.

**Theorem 2 (first event with fixed endpoints).** There is no J10 at any time
0 <= t <= T_n. The exact first J10 is at e_n, at (1,4), heading E. Its outgoing
state has head (1,5), heading N. The maximal y-coordinate visited before this
first event is exactly 4n+13.

Consequently both endpoints of the first-J10 macro are independent of n, while
its duration and its distance from the endpoint segment are unbounded. No fixed
endpoint-only radius or endpoint-only waiting bound exists for these macros.
The existing complete-input-hull bound is untouched: the input itself has
unbounded height as n grows.

**Theorem 3 (silent production of later defect fuel).** For 0 <= j <= n, define

    q_j = (1,4+4j),                 r_j = (0,7+4j).

Their following births and consumptions are CONSECUTIVE visits with turns R,L:

| Site | Birth time | Black consumption time | Lifetime age |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| q_j | 44+28j | 56n+168+20j | 56n+124-8j |
| r_j | 56n+146-28j | 56n+186+20j | 40+48j |

Every birth is strictly between zero and T_n, hence occurs with J10 FALSE.
Every displayed consumption is a J10 event. These are 2(n+1) DISTINCT physical
black lifetimes, not repeated counts of one bit. The q_j ages are all at least
48n+124; for each fixed D they therefore supply at least n+1 genuine J10 AND
S_D events when 48n+124>D.

In particular q_0 is written at the FIXED time 44 and consumed at the FIRST
J10, with age 56n+124 and no intervening J10 whatsoever. This rules out a uniform
new-black lifetime bound based just on the absence of intervening J10 events.
It does not rule out a bound depending on the full input, or one depending on
initial black mass (which is not fixed in this family).

Exactly 9n+6 J10 events occur in the interval occupied by P^(n+1). This is an
unbounded finite supply. Calling it amplification describes the observed and
proved count; it is not a claim of unique counterfactual causation by one event.

**Theorem 4 (the candidate pulse does NOT renew itself).** The first mismatch
with P^omega, starting at time T_n, is at relative time 20n+22: the actual turn
is L and the putative next pulse requires R. More generally, from any complete
finite black field of mass m at a P boundary, at most floor(m/2) whole copies
of P can execute consecutively. Thus P^omega is not a finite-support controller.
No claim about the eventual behaviour AFTER (2) is made for the entire family.

## 4. All-parameter physical coverage

This section is the finite-to-infinite bridge. Testing many values of n would
not prove (2). The verifier instead checks a complete arrangement of affine
integer intervals. Its domain has no parameter cutoff.

For any prescribed finite word, consecutive reads of the same physical cell
must alternate R,L. Matching the first requested read to the actual source
colour, then alternating, is also sufficient, by induction on update time.
This is the existing per-cell feasibility method, not a new criterion.

### 4.1 An exact representation of EVERY visit

In (2), only A and P are repeated a parameter-dependent number of times.
Their headings reset and their actual placed drifts are (0,4) or (0,-4).
Every other word is executed once. All horizontal positions are constants;
vertical starting positions are affine in n with coefficient divisible by four.

Partition cells as (x,4r+s), s in {0,1,2,3}. A phase of a repeated block is
visited at (x,4an+b+4epsilon*k), with epsilon=+1 or -1 and
0<=k<cn+d. Therefore that phase's presence at a fixed (x,s,r) is precisely
one half-open integer interval

    alpha*n+beta <= r < gamma*n+delta.                    (4)

A nonrepeated phase or an input template point gives a singleton interval.
Every visit phase of every block is retained. The compiler produces 274 read
phase tokens. This representation includes n=1, where A^(n-1) is empty.

A repeated-phase visit time is affine in n and r, since
k=epsilon*(r-an-b') and time=block_start+word_length*k+phase.
All visits within the same block have the same coefficients of n and r;
their order is fixed by the remaining constant. Distinct blocks are ordered
by their actual execution order. Thus no physical chronological ordering is
approximated or guessed from a drawing.

### 4.2 Finite arrangement, including the infinite parameter slab

Collect every affine endpoint in (4), including source, target and selected
lifetime intervals, in each (x,s) class. Endpoint order can change only where
two affine functions cross. For each rational crossing at or above one, insert
floor(crossing) and floor(crossing)+1 as integer slab starts. This isolates any
integer equality and both sides of every possible order reversal.

On each resulting n slab, sort endpoints and verify every adjacent ordering
for the ENTIRE slab. An affine inequality holds on a bounded integer interval
iff it holds at both endpoints; on an unbounded interval it additionally needs
a nonnegative slope. All arithmetic is exact integer or rational arithmetic.
Equal values of distinct endpoint functions are allowed only on an isolated
single-n slab, also checked.

Between successive ordered spatial endpoints, the set of active source points,
visits and target points is constant. Checking its ordered R/L list therefore
checks every cell in that band for EVERY n in its slab. Outside the outermost
endpoints all fields and visit lists are empty. All source and endpoint support
is included, so this proves COMPLETE field equality, not equality just where
the ant happens to read.

For this witness the resulting slabs are

    n=1; n=2; n=3; n>=4.

There are 30 (x,s) classes, 607 nonempty affine bands and 3,862 recorded colour
checks across the three checkpoint comparisons. These numbers describe a finite
certificate whose generic coverage was just proved; they are not an extrapolation
from running four seeds. In particular the last slab is checked all the way to
infinity by affine endpoint/slope conditions.

For each source-black band the verifier additionally requires a read in V_n.
Together with colour consistency, this proves extinction of every INITIAL black
lifetime before T_n. No site or historical field is erased to obtain this claim.

### 4.3 Lifetime identities

Add the selector intervals r=1,...,n+1 in classes (x,s)=(1,0) and (0,3). They
are split in the same complete arrangement. In each selected band the last read
in V_n is R and the first read in the P segment is L. Their times are checked
as affine identities over the WHOLE band, not just at a representative cell.
With j=r-1 these identities are exactly the table in Theorem 3.

For clarity, an affine identity f(n,r)=0 is checked at both spatial endpoints
r=left(n) and r=right(n)-1 and for all n in its slab. These become one-variable
affine identities; endpoint and slope checks suffice. Linearity then covers
every intervening r. The 27 selected affine bands verify both displayed families
and establish consecutiveness, not just two matching visit positions.

The per-cell argument proves (2), its complete fields, the extinction claim,
and the lifetime table. The literal endpoint geometry proves the checkpoint
poses and times. Source/profile disjointness yields the stated masses. QED.

## 5. First J10, event count, and geometric obstruction

All ten-factors of the words B, A and the junctions AA, AB, BA occur in the
finite cover B A A B A A. Each of its 307 ten-windows belongs to L10. Since
all blocks have length at least ten, this covers every junction in
B A^n B A^(n-1), for every n>=1. The true inverse past just proved supplies
exactly the first nine deleted letters. Thus all intrinsic windows during V_n
are legal. The final nine letters of A and B coincide, so the return history
is independent of n even when the final A run is empty.

Checking that fixed nine-letter suffix followed by two copies of P gives the
bad ending indices

    first P:       {1,2,3,4,5,19};
    later P:       {0,1,2,3,4,5,6,7,19}.

All later copies have the same nine-letter history. Consequently the first bad
index is T_n+1 and the P train contains 6+9n events. Both first-L fuel reads,
at indices 1 and 19 of every P, are among them. The first two turns R,L give
the claimed pre-/post-first-event poses.

Every affine phase interval before that event has y<=4n+13, and the second B
has a phase at y=4n+13. This proves the exact maximum. Because the input and
first-event endpoints are fixed, letting n grow refutes endpoint-only tube and
duration bounds. Likewise q_0 proves the zero-interior-J10 lifetime obstruction.
This does NOT contradict the S_D endpoint tube: there are intervening S_D reads,
including reads of initial black lifetimes on the long rail. Event segmentations
must not be interchanged.

## 6. Why the twenty-step pulse is not an infinite controller

P returns to the same heading with displacement (0,4), and every read position
in one copy has local height 0,1,2 or 3. Therefore the read footprints of distinct
copies are disjoint. Its first-L cells are exactly (1,0) and (0,3). Both must
already be black at the START of the entire proposed P train: no earlier copy
can have visited them. Each whole copy needs two distinct such cells, proving
the bound floor(m/2) for any finite complete source.

Each copy has net black growth four. This is compatible with consuming its two
specific required fuel cells: newly left black cells are behind the next read
band and do not supply the next copy's required first-L cells. Counting all
black cells is consequently not a count of remaining pulse fuel.

For the constructed input, (2) supplies n+1 copies. The following RLL agrees
with P on its first two letters, then differs: P[2]=R. Thus the first failure
is exactly 20(n+1)+2. This is a proved failure of the proposed pulse continuation,
not an implementation cap and not a proof of standardness after that time.

## 7. Consequence for the HC programme

The earlier plan to link J10 to remote old feedback needs this correction:

* A J10-free macro can make a long foldback and manufacture a large latent
  archive. Its full input geometry cannot be replaced by its two endpoints.
* Genuine later J10 fuel can be born entirely in J10-free periods. A literal
  event-written-only last-writer genealogy omits these physical dependencies.
* The manufactured archive is still finite for each seed. The demonstrated
  consumer does not rebuild the producer's input interface. No infinite chain
  has been constructed or excluded globally.

The remaining useful obligation is to control or realize the COMPOSITION of
archive-producing and archive-consuming episodes, retaining their full output
fields and verifying the next producer's input. A bound on how many times that
composition can renew, or a real infinite finite-seed composition, would address
the global issue. The present result repairs false premises on that route; it
is not reported as a new exclusion of an entire HC-counterexample branch.
