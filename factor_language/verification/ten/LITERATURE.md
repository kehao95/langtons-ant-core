# Primary-source check and priority boundary

Checked 15 October 2026. This is a targeted primary-source comparison, not a comprehensive bibliometric review or certification that no earlier result exists.

1. Anahí Gajardo, *A symbolic projection of Langton's Ant (Extended Abstract)*, DMTCS Proceedings AB, Discrete Models for Complex Systems (2003), pp.57–68. Primary PDF: https://dmtcs.episciences.org/en/articles/2312/download . Section 3, especially printed p.61, was read and visually inspected. It supplies the symbolic forbidden-word / consecutive-cell consistency framework. The present paper does not claim this method as new.
2. Anahí Gajardo, Victor Lutfalla, Michaël Rao, *Ants on the highway*, arXiv:2409.10124v1 (2024). Primary text: https://arxiv.org/html/2409.10124v1 . Used as background for highway certification and the distinction between finite-pattern evolution and full eventual behaviour. No general HC statement is imported from it.
3. Atharva Jillhewar, *Finite-Support Periodic Highways of Langton's Ant: Necessary Conditions, Transverse Exclusions, and Exact Search*, 2026 preprint, DOI 10.21203/rs.3.rs-10516269/v1. Author-hosted primary PDF: https://atharva12456.github.io/langtons-ant-highway/paper.pdf . Theorem 3.1 and Remark 3.2 on p.4 were read and visually inspected. Full-drift chronological translation classes and seed reconstruction are credited to this source. Other exclusion results in that manuscript are not dependencies of the new theorem.

Targeted searches for Langton-ant factor languages, eventual periodicity, transient black-count bounds, and ten-letter constraints did not identify the exact three-mode/ten-factor/cardinality-coefficient result in the inspected material. Several search hits were unrelated or secondary and were not used. A negative search result cannot establish priority.

The preceding project attachment *Sharp Factor-Language Rigidity of the Period-104 Langton Highway* is an unpublished companion research artifact. Its immediate-standardness thresholds 21/14/13 are NOT reclassified as new results here. Ten-factor eventual mode classification, the all-length folded-word obstruction, the sharp slope 12, and the nine-factor aperiodic construction are the new statements relative to that artifact.

Editorial assessment: a standalone central theorem is present, but a single stronger manuscript incorporating the earlier immediate-rigidity section may be preferable to publishing multiple substantially overlapping short notes. This is a research judgment, not an acceptance or novelty guarantee.
