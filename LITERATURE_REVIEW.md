## Contribution decisions

| **Memory 0.1.0** | Total next-old-black and birth-filtered queries with exact full-state output; quadratic event budgets and geometry; quantitative remote renewal necessity; a twenty-black, fixed-event-count lifetime obstruction. | Infinite memory effects are already discussed in symbolic-ant work. | **Port 0.2.0** | Sharp four-cell one-sided interface and 918-update three-cell bound; four-cell completion of arbitrary finite observations; protected finite scattering and return composition with complete history. | Earlier finite circuits already compose gadgets and finish in a highway seed. Explain this antecedent in the introduction; emphasize minimal resource threshold and the specific full-field closure theorem. |

The four-row theorem concerns ordinary finite rectangles with arbitrary initial
colours and internal poses. Jillhewar's transverse-width results concern
periodic plane highways and extremal guard rails. Their uses of “width four”
describe different domains and must not be interchanged.

The factor/memory event algorithms answer specific predicates on supplied finite
inputs. The defect theorem quantifies over coordinates at supplied budgets.
Neither is a claim that every reachability or eventual-periodicity problem for
the classical ant is decidable. Generalized multicolour rules and torus boundary
conditions are also separate models.
