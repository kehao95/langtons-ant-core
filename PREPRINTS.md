## Relationships between the results

The clock paper supplies general escape bounds and complete-state orbit
algorithms. Four-row escape proves an exact maximum for height four.
Wake studies periodic trajectories with specified drift and wake geometry.
Factor studies local turn languages, quantitative attraction and mass growth;
its finite-word mass argument is independent of its infinite attraction proof.
Defect synthesis studies complete-field arithmetic at a supplied defect budget.
Memory studies exact event prediction and the geometry of remote feedback.
Port studies minimal outgoing interfaces and composable finite scattering.
One-black covers every state with exactly one black cell.

See [literature and contributions](LITERATURE_REVIEW.md) for primary-source
comparisons and each article for its precise hypotheses.
