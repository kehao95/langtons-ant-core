integer induction supply the unbounded-width quantifier. The proof is analytic and computer-assisted; all full-field history is retained.

## Five-row memory and complete recovery

The [five-row argument](paper/five-row.tex) proves six complete local kernels,
a persistent unary recurrence, and autonomous composition. The round checkpoints
cover all counter lengths; the finite response catalogues supply the finite side
of the height-four/five threshold.

The [basin proof](paper/counter-basin.tex) covers nine unbounded parameter
programs and 59 finite bases. Exact affine sign partitions validate every read
and all future P104 epochs. Half-strip guards extend clipped counter inputs to
arbitrarily large aspect ratios while retaining every unused cell.

The [random-access proof](paper/random-access.tex) inverts the quadratic round
time by integer square root, evaluates powered words as finite XOR segments,
and restores the original unclipped field. Its uniform 20,000-term bound and
exact segment mass/equality algorithm imply polynomial bit complexity.
