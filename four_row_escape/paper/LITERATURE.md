# Literature and contribution review — 26 October 2026

The closest source is Kossi Roland Etse, *How Long Can The Escaping Ant Be
Confined?*, MFCS 2026, DOI [10.4230/LIPIcs.MFCS.2026.49](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.MFCS.2026.49).
Section 4.3.3, p. 49:14, states Conjecture 11; Figure 13 gives the lower
construction on p. 49:15. Published and arXiv theorem numbers differ.

The introduction now credits the existing lower family and computations.
The contribution is the complete persistent-response classification and
all-width exact upper certificate, with reconstructible attainment and
small-width values. Behavioral minimization and weighted recurrence are tools.
The original first-exit definition and all internal starting poses are retained.

This is ordinary rectangular escape. The diagonal transverse-width exclusions
in [Jillhewar's pinned paper](https://github.com/Atharva12456/langtons-ant-highway/blob/76a7e234e577d992d086c204c3dea00ae72cf10a/paper/main.tex)
concern periodic plane highways, a different problem.

Searches of the title, four-row escape and `34n` located no matching earlier
all-width upper proof. The dated portfolio review records search limits;
this is a literature comparison, not external mathematical review.
