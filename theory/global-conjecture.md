# Highway Conjecture

## Conjecture

For every finite black set, every ant position, and every heading, the
classical two-colour Langton ant eventually reaches a permanent member of the
standard P104 highway family, allowing translation and quarter-turn rotation.

The universal quantifier over finite support is essential. A successful search
through bounded boxes, a long trajectory, or a collection of low-support
families is not a proof of this statement.

## Proposed proof ladder

1. Establish a sound terminal predicate for the four P104 orientations.
2. Prove arbitrary-offset one-black termination through the prefix reduction
   and an entry-state obstacle theorem.
3. Establish a complete interaction language for multiple defects.
4. Prove that every nonterminal history either descends in a well-founded
   measure or is excluded by a finite, complete obstruction system.

At present, only the first item in an oriented blank form and a restricted
one-black family are promoted. The full conjecture remains open here.
