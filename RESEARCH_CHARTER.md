# Research Charter

## Objective

Study the classical two-colour ant on the square lattice from finite black
support.  The target is eventual membership in the standard P104 highway,
modulo translation, rotation, and phase.

## Research sequence

1. Define one exact state model and one fixed step convention.
2. Reconstruct a decidable terminal predicate for the standard P104 highway.
3. State the global conjecture and every restricted theorem with its exact
   quantifiers.
4. Build a complete-history renewal representation only after its preservation
   and completeness statements are explicit.
5. Pursue a global bridge: either a well-founded invariant for all legal
   renewal chains, or a uniform finite negative-certificate system.

## Evidence policy

A result is promoted only when this repository contains its precise statement,
its dependencies, its finite data when applicable, a narrow verifier, and a
description of the trusted computing base.  Simulations and search outputs may
guide research but never establish an infinite claim on their own.

## Reconstruction policy

This is not a file migration.  Material is reintroduced only after a human
review determines its role in the program.  A new implementation or proof must
be written against the new interfaces; copying predecessor source is out of
scope.
