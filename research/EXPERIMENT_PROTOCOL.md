# Experiment protocol

Exploration is valuable when it produces a falsifiable next question. Record
each run with the following fields:

1. the exact executable version and state convention;
2. the finite family of initial states or histories examined;
3. the stopping rule and every classification category;
4. the raw result or a content hash of it; and
5. the conjecture the result supports, challenges, or leaves untouched.

An experiment may propose a renewal invariant, find a counterexample to a
candidate, or prioritize a restricted family. It may not be cited as proof of
an unbounded statement without a separate completeness argument.

Promote a research result only by moving a deliberately prepared finite datum
into `evidence/` together with its checker and theorem-facing documentation;
do not promote scripts by declaration.
