# Proof and evidence map

The [complete article](paper/main.tex) provides all definitions and proofs.

| Claim | Universal argument | Finite evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Binomial prefix upper bound | Inject a turn prefix into the times of first reads of initially black cells | Exhaustive short-word time-code controls |
| Compactness and exact derivatives | Nested minimal sources; arbitrarily late fresh reads from the corner argument | Finite accumulation examples supplement the proof |
| Sharp exponent and positive subspace | Whole-history protected binary channel, finite marker subsets | Complete blank startup and exact 208/228-step kernels |
| Optimal mistake budget | Predict repeated reads exactly and a fresh read white; match by the protected channel | Local encoding checks |

The trace spaces are anchored at a fixed initial pose. Their topological rank
is a property of the space of infinite observations. The positive channel
reuses the attributed one-black scattering response, whose complete local data
and guards are reproduced in the article. The new article's analytic proofs
and exact local data are self-contained in this package.
