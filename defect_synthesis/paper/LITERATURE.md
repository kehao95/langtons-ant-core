# Literature and contributions

The contribution combines a complete bounded-defect physical cover with exact
mutable-field arithmetic, endpoint reconstruction, explicit resource spectra
and unary-budget complexity. The restricted QF backend additionally encodes
spatial coincidences, intra-loop chronology and XOR writes. Its source/stride
restrictions and distinction from full-target equality remain explicit.

The arithmetic argument uses the following established results:

| Input | Primary source and location |
|---|---|
| Presburger–semilinear equivalence | [Ginsburg–Spanier (1966)](https://msp.org/pjm/1966/16-2/pjm-v16-n2-p09-p.pdf), Theorem 1.3 |
| Unary counting elimination | [Schweikardt](https://arxiv.org/pdf/cs/0211022), Theorem 5.4; TOCL 6 (2005), DOI 10.1145/1071596.1071602 |
| Piecewise-affine definable functions | [Cluckers (2003)](https://rcluckers.perso.math.cnrs.fr/prints/Presb-P-min.pdf), Theorem 1 |
| Flat-run schemas and NP arguments | [Demri–Dhar–Sangnier (2015)](https://www.irif.fr/~sangnier/publis/DDS-IC15.pdf), Theorem 8.4 |
| QF counting encodings | [Decker–Pirogov (2019)](https://arxiv.org/html/1901.05692v1), Section 4, Theorems 4.1–4.2 |
| Integer small solutions | [Bottesch et al. (2020)](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-030-55754-6_14), p. 242 and Theorem 11 |

The unary-counting lemma is recalled background. The finite-portfolio supply
dichotomy is a general semilinear consequence. The exact ant-specific image
and sharp `10D<=9C-309` family remain substantive results. NP membership uses
the full physical cover and polynomial-size verification, not just generic
Presburger decidability. The factor paper owns the bounded-fold coverage input;
[Gajardo (2003)](https://dmtcs.episciences.org/articles/2312) supplies the symbolic
word context.
