1. Account for every turn and every black cell

There are 84 distinct cyclic length-ten factors in P104. For any finite black initial field and interval [s, t), let N₁₀ count violating ten-turn windows lying wholly inside that interval. The mass–defect theorem states

What makes this an all-input inequality?

The proof enumerates physically feasible finite legal words and checks integer potential inequalities on a length-300 graph with 29,334 vertices and 29,559 edges. It does not assume that a legal finite word can extend to an infinitely legal trajectory. Splitting at violating windows gives the penalty 351. The 90-step witness realizes the exact constant 322 with zero violations; 351 is not asserted optimal.

2. See why the attraction slope must be 12

Under the additional promise that every future ten-factor is legal, a finite field of mass m reaches the full standard trace by τ ≤ 12m + 212. This explicit family spends longer and longer in a legal transient: mₙ = 2n + 13 and τₙ = 24n − 3.

From a local language to eventual standard motion

The physical factor graph has three recurrent possibilities: periods 104, 28 and 24. An all-parameter collision argument excludes arbitrary interleaved folds in its non-simple component. Infinite backgrounds can support the latter patterns; finite black support excludes them. Eventual periodicity is the conclusion, not an assumption.

3. Legal motion can manufacture long-lived memory

A legal stretch can write a black cell that will be consumed much later. This complete seed comes with its actual nine-turn past. Changing n lengthens a ribbon while preserving the exact birth and return formulas.

Two proof chains, with distinct premises

Infinite-future legality → quantitative attraction

Local language → physically feasible graph → all-parameter fold exclusions → recurrent alternatives → finite-support elimination → τ ≤ 12m + 212. The exact event algorithm can decide the next J₁₀ and return a complete compressed endpoint. Deciding each next event does not prove that the whole event chain terminates.

Arbitrary finite words → mass–defect bound

Physical feasibility → length-300 integer potential → all-length legal finite-word bound → split at violations → the inequality above. This proof is independent of the infinite-future attraction theorem.

Complete finite constructions → sharpness and memory costs

Exact rail cancellation proves the sharp attraction family for every n. Affine read-interval checks prove silent memory manufacture and recovery/reset results. Keeping the entire field makes later readback visible; deleting the history would destroy these conclusions.
