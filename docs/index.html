02 / FROM INTERACTION TO PROOF

Three ways to look closely.

Run the exact rule.

Replays turn, flip, and move on the complete finite field. Controls let you inspect a specific orbit, its true past, and the quantities that change.

Inspect a certificate.

Source-pinned data exposes finite witnesses, exact inequalities, and complete classifications. Each lab links to the code that regenerates its data.

Follow the general argument.

The proof steps explain coverage beyond any displayed example. The one-black theorem is Lean-checked; other results combine analytic arguments with the exact checks described in their papers.

Each theorem keeps its stated input class. Together these results illuminate the finite-support Highway Conjecture, which remains an open research question. All eight preprints and their published versions are listed in the publication portfolio.
